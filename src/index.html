Textures
Pictures from Mario Bros. Circuit, Bowser's Castle, Rainbow Road, Desert Hills and DK Spaceport, read from your dump. They follow the road round bends. The number is how many metres one copy covers (empty = its usual size, 0 = stretched across).
Boost panels and glider ramps
The game's boost panel, glide panel and glider ramp, built into the road's model with their own collision (boost on the panel's arrows; the ramp launches you into a glide off its top, like the game's), so no map object is needed. Ramps and glide panels also get a glide path (the light blue line and boxes): where gliding karts and CPUs fly; set how far it goes with glide. Pick one, press Place and click on the road; change where it sits with the numbers (metres along the road from the start line, and metres left of the middle; negative is right).
World terrain
The open world's real ground, read from your game files, so you can line your track up with it. It's the ground karts actually drive on (the game's collision), so Exact matches the game to the centimetre. Only the area you choose is read; the lower details trade accuracy for fewer triangles over big areas. It's just for reference: it isn't added to your track.

Rail paths (the game's RailSlidePath) along your model's rails, fence tops, pipes or edges: karts can grind along them. Press Draw a rail, then click along the top of the rail in your model, in order (Backspace takes the last point back, Enter or Done finishes, Esc cancels). How to use it

  1. Open RomFS… and pick your dump's romfs folder. In Chrome or Edge it saves straight into the recomp. Other browsers (Firefox, Brave, Safari) ask for just the Banc folder and save the mod as a .zip instead.
  2. Pick a course from the list. Race_… files are the race layouts; battle and connecting-road files are there too.
  3. Click objects in the 3D view or the list on the left. Drag the arrows to move them, or type exact numbers on the right.
  4. Add object: click a name in the bottom-right list to drop it at the centre of the view. Scan all reads every course once so you can add any object the game uses (it's remembered afterwards).
  5. Paths: the green line is the lap path (with checkpoints), blue lines are CPU/route paths, and the faint lines either side show the road width. Their points can be moved, duplicated and deleted like objects.
  6. Save as mod and pick the recomp's mods folder (mkw-recomp\mods). It writes mods\<name>\romfs\…, the manifest and mod.json, and updates the resource size table when a course grows. Then enable the mod in the recomp's Mods page. Edits to several courses in one session all go into the same mod.
  7. Quick Create lays out a whole course from one guide line. Click Draw guide, then click along the road in driving order from the start line (Enter when done), or use the course's existing lap path. It previews a lap path with checkpoints, CPU routes, rows of item boxes and an optional 24-kart start grid; Create adds them all in one step (Ctrl+Z undoes it).
  8. Your own models: in the Track Creator pick Road from: My own model and import an OBJ, FBX or DAE, plus (optionally) a simpler collision model. Use Start grid .obj to line it up: 1 unit = 1 metre, Y up, the race starts at the origin heading -Z. Pick its .mtl and texture images along with it and the textures go into the game too (hover a material's picture to enlarge it, click it to choose another image). Pick what kind of ground each material is. Then use Quick Create as usual (Draw guide along your road → Create): it lays out the lap path, CPU routes, item boxes and start grid on your model, and the start grid goes wherever your guide starts. The centre line still lays out the lap path, CPU route and items, so drag it along your road.
  9. My track (top bar): makes a brand-new track. Its editors are the tabs over the view, like Track Studio: Track (name, cup, music, pictures, Free Roam, Build), Model, Collision, Paths and Map (objects and every path). The Track Creator makes a brand-new track in its own slot (for now it's raced as Mushroom Cup race 1). Drag the yellow points, use Add points and click the ground to add more, set a point's height for hills, then Build mod. You need the whole RomFS folder open, because the track is built from your game files.
  10. Track model: when a course opens, its road model is loaded from your dump and drawn in grey/green/brown (textures aren't shown). With Snap to ground ticked, objects you drag, add or create with Quick Create sit on it; End or Drop to track puts the selection on it. Double-click the track to orbit around that spot.
  11. Minimap… draws the course's minimap from its lap paths (road width from each point), framed the way the game frames it, and puts it in the mod when you save. Tick Re-frame if your new layout spills off the edge.
  12. Branching CPU routes: click a route point and press Draw branch from here, click the new line, and finish near another route point to join back (Enter). Or select two points and press Branch 1st → 2nd, then drag the new points into place. Link/Unlink connect or disconnect two points; pink lines show links.
  13. Collision of deleted objects: some objects (signs, arrows, walls…) have their collision baked into the course's collision files. When you delete one, Save as mod switches that collision off in the course's Race_… / RaceStage_… collision files as well. Moving a baked object doesn't move its collision.
  14. Opening a course that one of your mods already changed offers to open that edited version, so you can keep working on it.
  15. For an emulator (Eden / Eglantine / PokeDinner) use Download file and put it in <mod>\romfs\Banc\.

The road surface itself isn't drawn: in Mario Kart World the terrain lives in the shared world map, not the course file. The paths show where the road runs. Mod names can't contain spaces: the recomp cuts the name at the first space.

Open the Banc folder

This browser can't open whole folders for editing (only Chrome and Edge can), and your romfs has over 200,000 files. So pick just the Banc folder inside it:

(your dump)\romfs\Banc

If the browser asks whether to "upload" the files, say yes: nothing leaves your computer, the page only reads them. Saving gives you a mod .zip to extract into mkw-recomp\mods.

Optional: also load romfs\System\Resource\ResourceSizeTable.Product.100.Nin_Ounce_NVN2.rsizetable.zs. It's only needed if your edits make a course file bigger, and you'll be asked for it then.

One more file needed

Your changes made a course file bigger than the original, so the game's size table has to be updated too or the course may crash on load. Please pick:

romfs\System\Resource\ResourceSizeTable.Product.100.Nin_Ounce_NVN2.rsizetable.zs
